🛡️ WHAT DEFENDER IS
A fast, honest first look at your security posture. Zero third-party dependencies (Python standard library only), so it's small enough to read, audit, and trust. Self-hosted by design. And deliberately scoped as triage — it tells you where to look and what to fix first. It runs alongside a heavier EDR/SIEM, or stands in while a small team has nothing else. We'll always tell you where it ends and human judgment begins.

🔍 THE SEVEN DETECTION MODULES
• Log Analyzer — spots brute-force, credential-stuffing, and the inhuman cadence of automated recon in your auth, access, and app logs.
• Phishing / Content — scores messages for the linguistic markers of AI-generated social engineering (the "bad grammar" tell is dead).
• Endpoint Posture — read-only self-scan of open ports, missing controls, processes, and live connections on the host.
• LLM / Prompt Defense — catches prompt-injection, jailbreaks, and data exfiltration in traffic to and from your AI systems.
• Network Baseline — learns normal, then flags beacons, lateral movement, and slow exfil that deviate from it.
• Vuln & CVE Audit — matches your exposure against a CVE index and IP-reputation data to surface known-exploitable gaps.
• Mitigation Advisor — ranks every fix by impact ÷ effort, with a grounded Q&A you can interrogate.

Every alert cites the exact rule, baseline, or CVE that triggered it. No unexplained "AI says bad."

⚡ WHY SELF-HOSTED MATTERS
A security tool shouldn't make you ship your most sensitive logs to a stranger's cloud. Defender runs locally; results stay local. The only outbound traffic is optional CVE/reputation feed updates — and you control whether that happens at all. You can run it fully offline.

🧠 THE AI-ATTACK ERA, HONESTLY
"AI-powered attack detection" means two concrete things here: (1) detecting attacks that are themselves AI-assisted — automated recon, machine-written phishing, credential attacks at machine speed; and (2) defending your own AI systems against prompt injection, jailbreaks, and exfiltration. It does NOT mean an inscrutable model making verdicts you can't question. Every detection is rule-, baseline-, or data-based, and fully explainable.
